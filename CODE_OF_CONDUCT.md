# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want TinyMind to be a welcoming project for anyone interested in small,
efficient, local AI systems — from first-time contributors to people
building production mobile deployments. We pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback on code and design.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming malice or incompetence.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions)
and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers listed in the repository's
public home once one exists. Reports will be reviewed and addressed
promptly and fairly. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions
and comments that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate,
threatening, or harmful.

This document may be revised as the project and community grow.
